# Niche profiles

These profiles inform the DESIGN NOTES section. Each profile lists what buyers in that niche expect, what builds trust, and what's a red flag.

Add new niches by following the same template. PRs welcome.

---

## PLUMBING / HVAC / TRADES

**Buyer mindset:** Often urgent (water on the floor, no AC in July). Decision is usually "who do I trust enough to call right now."

**Trust signals (above the fold):**
- Real photos of real techs in branded uniforms (NOT stock images of wrenches)
- Local phone number, click-to-call, large
- Service area named explicitly ("serving Tucson and the East Valley")
- License number visible
- Reviews count + star rating ("4.9 stars · 800+ reviews")
- 24/7 / Same-day badge if applicable

**Red flags:**
- Stock photos
- "Get a quote" form as primary CTA (vs phone)
- No service area mentioned (looks like a national lead-gen site)
- Logo-centered layout (feels DIY blog, not established business)
- Sliders / carousels (nobody reads slide 2)

**Convention:** Left-aligned logo, phone number top-right, large click-to-call hero CTA.

## DENTAL / MEDICAL / HEALTHCARE

**Buyer mindset:** Anxious. Researching. Comparing 3-5 providers. Insurance is a top concern.

**Trust signals:**
- Doctor's photo, name, credentials (MD, DDS, etc.)
- Insurance accepted, listed clearly
- "New patient special" or new-patient flow
- Reviews, especially Google
- Years in practice, board certifications
- Office photos (modern, clean)

**Red flags:**
- Generic stock smiles
- No doctor name or face
- Insurance buried in footer
- Outdated design (signals outdated practice)
- No "what to expect on your first visit" content

**Convention:** Trust-forward, calm color palette (blues, soft greens), doctor's name and face above the fold.

## RESTAURANT / HOSPITALITY

**Buyer mindset:** Decision happens fast. Hungry. Wants menu, hours, location, photos. Usually on mobile.

**Trust signals:**
- Menu accessible in 1 tap (NOT a PDF download)
- Hours, address, phone in header
- Real food photos (theirs, not stock)
- Reservation link or "Order Online" CTA
- Recent reviews

**Red flags:**
- Menu as PDF
- Auto-playing music or video
- Slow loading (you'll never see this customer)
- Stock food photos
- Flash splash page (if anyone still has these)

**Convention:** Photo-forward, large hero image of signature dish, immediate access to menu/hours/location.

## SALON / SPA / PERSONAL CARE

**Buyer mindset:** Visual decision. Wants to see the work. Booking convenience matters a lot.

**Trust signals:**
- Portfolio / before-and-after gallery
- Online booking (NOT "call to schedule")
- Stylist/practitioner bios with photos
- Service menu with prices (or starting prices)
- Instagram embed (proves they're active and current)

**Red flags:**
- No portfolio
- No online booking
- No prices anywhere
- Stale Instagram (if embedded)
- Outdated portfolio photos

**Convention:** Visual, portfolio-driven, online-booking CTA prominent.

## LAW FIRM

**Buyer mindset:** High-stakes, often distressed (injury, divorce, criminal). Comparing reputations.

**Trust signals:**
- Attorney name, photo, credentials
- Practice areas clearly listed
- Case results / settlements (if ethically permitted in jurisdiction)
- "Free consultation" CTA
- Bar associations, awards
- Local courthouse familiarity

**Red flags:**
- Stock gavel/scales imagery
- No attorney name above the fold
- Generic practice area lists with no specialization
- Aggressive "WE FIGHT FOR YOU" without substance

**Convention:** Authoritative, conservative palette (navy, gold, deep red), attorney portraits, results-focused.

## REAL ESTATE AGENT

**Buyer mindset:** Looking for local expertise. Comparing agents. Wants recent listings.

**Trust signals:**
- Recent sold listings with prices
- Agent's photo, brokerage, MLS#
- Neighborhood pages / local guides
- Testimonials from recent buyers/sellers
- IDX search

**Red flags:**
- No recent listings (looks inactive)
- Stock house photos
- No neighborhood content
- Brokerage logo bigger than agent

**Convention:** Big agent photo, search bar prominent, recent transactions visible.

## FITNESS / GYM / STUDIO

**Buyer mindset:** Trial-driven. Wants to know vibe, schedule, and price before committing.

**Trust signals:**
- "Free trial / first class free" CTA
- Real photos of real members (diverse, not all 22-year-old models)
- Class schedule visible
- Pricing or "starting at $X"
- Trainer bios

**Red flags:**
- All shredded model photos
- No schedule
- No pricing (signals "we're expensive")
- Generic motivational quotes

---

## E-COMMERCE / DTC

**Buyer mindset:** Comparison shopping. Wants product details, reviews, shipping info.

**Trust signals:**
- Multiple product photos (lifestyle + detail)
- Reviews with photos
- Clear shipping/return policy
- Trust badges (secure checkout, etc.)
- Size guide / fit info
- Stock count / urgency (where authentic)

**Red flags:**
- One blurry photo
- No reviews
- Hidden shipping cost
- Sketchy checkout flow

---

## SAAS / B2B

**Buyer mindset:** Evaluating. Wants to know what it does in 5 seconds, then dig into specifics.

**Trust signals:**
- Clear value prop in hero ("X for Y")
- Product screenshot or video
- Customer logos
- Pricing page (or "Talk to sales" with clear ICP)
- Documentation / changelog
- Founder/team page

**Red flags:**
- Vague hero ("Transform your workflow")
- No product visual
- Hidden pricing AND no clear ICP
- No customer logos / social proof

## GENERIC

Use this when the niche isn't covered above.

**Universal trust signals:**
- Clear value prop in hero
- Phone / email / address visible
- Reviews or testimonials
- Real photos of real people
- Mobile-friendly

**Universal red flags:**
- Stock photos
- No contact info above the fold
- Carousel as hero
- Auto-play media
- Slow load
- No clear next action
